# Flood animation: water filling up and rain falling

Objective: show the DG2/FV1 run as an animation in which the accumulation zones fill little by little and the rain is visibly falling, and make the depth distribution understandable.
Branch: `feature/flood-ui-dg2`. Delivery: work-unit commits. Runners: `pytest`, `manage.py test` (PYTHONPATH=..), `node --test`, `tsc -b`, eslint, Playwright flow check.

## Specs

- S1 "agregues la animación de que se va rellenando de poco en poco las zonas donde se va acumulando agua": playback of the run frames (play/pause, time slider with simulated time, speed), smooth transitions, enough frames for a fluid animation.
- S2 "además, de como se ve que esta lloviendo": a rain effect on the map while it rains in the simulated time, its strength following the hyetograph; respects reduced-motion.
- S3 "Checa bien lo de las profundidades y como se distribuye el agua, porque son varios milimetros que llovio en esa simulación y no es posible que solo se concentre en zonas especificias": thin runoff sheets (millimetres) must be visible during the rain, and the results must explain where the water went (rain, infiltrated, on the slopes, ponded, left the domain).
- S5 "esta animación quiero que se reproduzca mientras se esperan los resultados, como una barra de progreso, para que el usuario vaya viendo como va el progreso y que zonas se van inundando": while the run is in progress, the panel shows the simulated time reached (progress bar) and the frames already written by the solver as they appear, with the rain effect; when the run ends it switches to the full playback.
- S6 "Si, pero que se pueda ver que llovio en toda la zona, haz mas presente eso": a visible "superficie mojada" class from 0.5 mm so the whole area shows wet while it rains and drying afterwards, and the rain over the whole ROI made more present on the map.
- S4 Keep existing behaviour and contracts working for older runs; results stay "no validados".
